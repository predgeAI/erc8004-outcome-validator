Support Predge open source

Predge publishes its verification tools as MIT-licensed open source, maintained by a solo developer. If they are useful to you, a crypto donation helps keep them maintained. Donations cover RPC and hosting costs, test vectors and ongoing maintenance. A donation does not buy a service, a token or any special treatment.

Before you send

  • These are the same addresses Predge already uses to receive x402 payments on its API.
  • Send USDC or the network's native token only. Other tokens may not be recoverable.
  • Check the first and last characters of the address in your wallet before confirming.
